About Aalaya Aqua Care

Clean water solutions from a trusted local RO specialist.

Aalaya Aqua Care is a Sirkali-based sales and service centre for RO, UV and UF water purifiers, iron removers, and water softeners. We also handle electrical, plumbing, and air compressor work, including clearing blocked pipelines.
